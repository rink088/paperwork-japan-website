Paperwork Japan

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. About This App

Paperwork Japan helps users understand Japanese documents through optical character recognition (OCR), AI-powered explanations, and multilingual translation.

2. Information We Process

When you use the app, we may process document images you select, Japanese text recognized from those images, your selected language, and information required to provide the requested AI service.

Documents may contain personal or sensitive information. Please avoid submitting information that is not necessary for your request.

3. AI Processing

Document images or extracted text are transmitted over encrypted connections to our Cloudflare backend and forwarded to Google Gemini to generate explanations or translations.

Google processes submitted information according to its applicable service terms and data handling policies. We recommend reviewing Google's privacy information before submitting sensitive documents.

4. Device Security and Usage Records

We use Apple App Attest and Cloudflare services to verify app requests, manage anonymous sessions, prevent misuse, and enforce daily AI request limits.

Our backend maintains technical records such as device attestation identifiers and public keys, hashed session tokens, session timestamps, and request usage records. These records help operate and protect the service.

5. Document Storage and Local History

Our application database is designed to maintain security and usage records rather than store document images or extracted document text.

The app may save limited information about recent explanations on your device, such as document titles, dates, urgency, and deadlines. You can clear this history within the app.

6. Diagnostics

Technical error information may be recorded to diagnose service problems, including HTTP status codes and AI service error categories. Cloudflare and other infrastructure providers may process operational metadata under their applicable policies.

7. Advertising and Tracking

Paperwork Japan does not currently display third-party advertisements or use information for cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell personal information.

8. Data Retention and Requests

Security and usage records may be retained as needed for service operation, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the record and service provider.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information associated with your use of the app, contact us at the address below. We may need additional information to identify the relevant technical records.

9. Children

Paperwork Japan is not intended for children.

10. Limitations

AI explanations and translations may contain mistakes. Paperwork Japan is not a substitute for professional legal, immigration, tax, medical, or financial advice.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
