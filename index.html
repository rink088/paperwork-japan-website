Frequently Asked Questions

How do I explain a Japanese document?

Open the app, scan a document or choose a photo, select your language, and tap Explain with AI.

How can I translate Japanese text?

Select a document photo, wait for text recognition, then tap Translate to your selected language.

Why does the app say I reached my AI limit?

Paperwork Japan currently allows up to five AI requests per device per day. Try again the next day.

Are AI explanations always correct?

No. AI-generated explanations may contain mistakes. Verify important information with the original document or issuing organization.

Troubleshooting

If a request fails, check your internet connection, restart the app, and try again. If the issue continues, contact support with a description of the problem. Please do not email sensitive documents or identification details.
